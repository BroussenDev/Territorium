# Territorium — Changelog

## v0.1.14

🌍 **Nations, clearer**

- In Nations games, players now show as "Country | Name", so you know who holds which country at a glance.
- When you lose your capital, a banner tells you how long until you can proclaim a new one, then that you can do it now.
- The country picker has continent filters: pick Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America or Oceania to highlight its countries on the map and list them all.

🔁 **Resume my game**

- Closed the tab or lost your connection? The home page now offers to take you back into the multiplayer game you were playing, as long as it's still running.

📊 **End-of-game recap**

- The end-of-game screen now tells you about your game: your peak size and share of the map, your most troops, your best minute, how many attacks you launched, how long you played, and who eliminated you.

## v0.1.13

©️ **Territorium's own creations**

- Territorium's original icons (MIRV, EMP, radar, white flag, medals, emeralds), sounds and store cosmetics now sit with the Territorium brand, owned by Broussen Studio. Nothing changes in the game.

## v0.1.12

👑 **Capitals stand out**

- In Nations games, every capital now wears a gold crown on the map, so you can spot at a glance which city to defend and which one to take.
- Capitals can no longer be upgraded. A city proclaimed as the new capital keeps the level it already had.

## v0.1.11

🌍 **New game mode: Nations**

- A new "Game modes" section on the home page, with a first mode: Nations, a multiplayer game for up to 80 players on the giant world map.
- In the lobby, pick a real country on the world map. Each country shows its capital and its share of the world's land; countries already taken are greyed out. Players who pick nothing get a random free country.
- Everyone starts with their whole country, borders included. Every country nobody picked is played by the AI. Antarctica stays neutral.
- Small countries start with extra gold and troops (up to 400K gold and 75K troops) so every pick stays playable.
- Own 50% of the land to win.

🏛️ **Capitals**

- Each country starts with a free city on its real capital. While you hold it, you get +10% gold and +10% troop growth.
- If your capital falls, every player is told, and your troops defend 10% worse for one minute.
- Two minutes later, click one of your cities and choose "Make this city my capital city" to proclaim a new one, for free.

## v0.1.10

⚓ **Warship limit**

- Each player can now own at most 50 warships at once. Once you reach the limit, the warship button is greyed out until one of your ships is sunk.
- Hover over the warship button to see your fleet size (for example "Fleet: 12/50 warships").

## v0.1.9

🏳️ **Peace only between enemies**

- You can now only offer peace to a player you are at war with: one of you is attacking the other, or you fought in the last 2 minutes (land taken, attack launched, missile fired or perjury). Otherwise the white flag is greyed out.
- A proposal you received can still be signed after the fighting has stopped.

🔔 **Event history**

- A bell next to the timer, top right, opens the history of your game: who attacked you, who fired a missile at you, who you signed peace or an alliance with, trade, chat… Each line shows how long ago it happened ("2 min ago").
- Filter by attacks, missiles, diplomacy, trade or chat. Click a line to go to the player or the unit. A badge counts what you haven't seen yet.

🎯 **Objectives appear after one minute**

- Map objectives are now hidden for the first minute of the game, so no one can rush them from their spawn. The control panel counts down to them.
- When they appear, every player gets an announcement and a sound.

🏗️ **Real building upgrades**

- Levels are no longer written on the map. An upgraded building shows its frame instead: bronze at level 2, silver at 3, gold at 4 and a new emerald frame at 5.
- Every upgradable building now has a maximum level. Cities, ports and factories go up to level 5, right away. SAM launchers, silos and radars keep their caps and unlock times.
- The building card shows what the next level really brings: max population for a city, trade ships and warship repair docks for a port, trains for a factory.
- The build bar now counts your buildings, not their levels.
- Nations build a new city, port or factory once all of theirs have reached level 5.

🎡 **New radial menu**

- The right-click menu is redrawn in Territorium's style: rounded slices with a gold rim, a gold dial around the ring and a hatched look on actions you can't use.

🪙 **Territorium coin**

- The coin test under the radial menu is over: it has been removed.

## v0.1.8

🏳️ **Peace treaties**

- Two players at war can now sign peace without becoming allies. Right-click an enemy (or open their player panel) and choose the white flag. They get 15 seconds to sign or refuse.
- Once signed, neither side can attack the other for 3 minutes: land attacks turn back, boats sail home and missiles in flight between you are called off. Trade embargoes between you are lifted.
- Breaking the peace is perjury. It costs what betraying an ally costs (traitor mark, half defense for 30 seconds), every player is told, and you cannot offer peace to your victim again for 10 minutes. Firing a missile or an EMP at a player you are at peace with breaks it too.
- After a refusal, you must wait 60 seconds before asking that player again. When a peace runs out, either side can offer a new one after 30 seconds. You can only have one proposal waiting at a time.
- Nations weigh each offer: they refuse traitors, keep pressing a war they are winning, and gladly accept peace from a stronger player. Tribes accept every offer.
- The player panel shows how long the peace has left, and the timer ring on the "Break peace" button in the radial menu shows it too.

🪙 **Territorium coin**

- The Territorium coin now sits under the radial menu when you right-click a country. It is a visual test for now.

## v0.1.5

🏗️ **Building upgrades**

- Click one of your buildings to open its card: its level, what the next level brings, and an Upgrade button with the price.
- Upgraded buildings get a metal frame on the map and on the card: bronze at level 2, silver at level 3, gold from level 4.
- Military buildings now have a level cap, and each level unlocks after a set time, counted from the end of the spawn phase. While a level is locked, the card counts down to it.
  - SAM launcher: up to level 5. Level 2 at 5 min, level 3 at 10 min, level 4 at 15 min, level 5 at 20 min.
  - Missile silo: up to level 3. Level 2 at 5 min, level 3 at 10 min.
  - Radar: it can now be upgraded, up to level 3 (level 2 at 5 min, level 3 at 10 min). Each level adds 25 tiles of range: 100, 125, then 150.
- In ranked games the caps are lower and levels come later: SAM up to level 3, silo and radar up to level 2, level 2 at 8 min and level 3 at 15 min.
- Cities, ports and factories are unchanged: no cap, no wait.

## v0.1.4

⚡ **EMP bomb**

- An EMP now bursts on impact: a white flash, then a ring of blue lightning and crackling sparks that spreads over its whole 20-tile reach. An EMP shot down by a SAM explodes like any other intercepted missile.

🧱 **Control panel**

- The build bar is split into four trays (economy, defense, navy, missiles) and fills the panel's width. Each tile shows its hotkey in the corner and how many you own under the icon, or the price for missiles. The building you're placing is framed in gold.
- On computers, the map objectives now sit next to the attack slider instead of taking a row of their own.

## v0.1.3

🧱 **Build bar**

- Buildings are now grouped: economy (city, factory, port), defense (defense post, missile silo, SAM launcher, radar), navy (warship) and missiles (atom bomb, hydrogen bomb, EMP bomb, MIRV), with a thin separator between each group. The build menu and the hotkey settings follow the same order.
- New default hotkeys: `V` for the radar and `X` for the EMP bomb. The old ones (`=` and `-`) also zoomed the map.

⚡ **EMP**

- A structure hit by an EMP is now shown in steady red for as long as it is disabled, instead of pulsing.

🛡️ **Reports and game history**

- Player reports now reach the moderation team, who review each one with the game's replay.
- Your game history and replays are kept for 30 days (subscribers keep theirs longer, as before). A game that is under review is kept until the review is done.

## v0.1.2

⚡ **EMP bomb**

- A new missile fired from a missile silo, for 1,500,000 gold (fixed price). Hotkey: `-`.
- It does no damage and takes no land. On impact it disables every enemy defense post, SAM launcher, missile silo, port and radar within 20 tiles for 15 seconds. Disabled structures pulse electric blue.
- A disabled SAM doesn't fire, a disabled silo can't launch, a disabled port sends no ships and a disabled defense post gives no defense bonus.
- Once a structure recovers, it is immune to EMPs for 30 seconds, so it can't be kept down forever.
- Your own and your allies' structures are never affected. SAM launchers can shoot an EMP down like any other missile.

📡 **Radar**

- A new structure: 250,000 gold for the first, +250,000 for each one you own, capped at 1,000,000. It takes 5 seconds to build. Hotkey: `=`.
- It sweeps twice per second and spots enemy boats, warships and missiles within 100 tiles. You get a message and a sonar ping, once per unit. Your own and allied units are ignored.
- An EMP blinds a radar while it is disabled.

## v0.1.0

🎯 **Map objectives**

- A few round zones appear on the map in every solo, private and public game (a checkbox when you create the game turns them off). Ranked, 1v1, 2v2 and the daily solo race don't have them.
- Hold most of a zone's land for 15 seconds to capture it. Each zone you hold gives +15% worker gold and +5% troop growth, until someone takes it from you.
- Your bonus counts at most half the zones (2 of 3 or 4, 3 of 5 or 6). Holding more keeps them from your rivals but pays nothing extra.
- Each zone has a gold name ("◆ Objective A"). For the first five minutes, zones pulse and show their bonus. You can hide the names in the settings (Objective Names).
- The control panel shows how many zones you hold and your bonus.

🏆 **Ranked seasons**

- Ranked is now a free-for-all of 8 to 16 signed-in players on a fixed pool of maps, with no bots, nations or cosmetics.
- The ranked hub shows the current season, your tier and Elo, your placement games and the tier list.
- The leaderboard has a Ranked tab. Profiles show your standing this season and the badges from past seasons.

📅 **Challenges**

- New Challenges page: daily, weekly and monthly challenges, with progress bars and medal rewards.
- The solo race of the day: everyone plays the same map from the same start. The fastest win ranks first, and the server replays each win to check it.

📊 **Leaderboard and profiles**

- New Solo tab that ranks signed-in players by their solo wins against nations. The old ranked tab is now Multiplayer.
- Profiles show an Admin or Moderator badge for staff.

🔒 **Privacy**

- No more third-party ad script. YouTube videos load (without cookies) only when you click them.

✨ **More**

- Only clan members can wear the clan tag.
- The in-game scoreboard and HUD no longer stay on the home page after you leave a game.
- The home page is lighter: the closed store no longer redraws in the background.

# Territorium — Changelog

## v0.1.79

🛡️ **New mode: Survival**

- Every player on the same side against 10 waves of AI invaders, a new one every 2 minutes 30, each bigger than the last.
- The invaders never cheat: they start like a player who joins and play by exactly the same rules as you (troops, growth, buildings), whatever the difficulty. They get stronger only by their numbers.
- Wipe out the last wave to win; the game is lost when no defender is left. A banner shows the current wave and the time before the next one.

## v0.1.78

🏆 **Badges on the leaderboards and the scoreboard**

- Your badge now appears next to your name on the leaderboards (Ranked, Solo and 1v1/2v2) and in the in-game scoreboard.

## v0.1.77

🎖️ **Choose your badge**

- A new button next to your name lets you choose the badge shown in lobbies, or none. Your choice follows you on every PC with Steam Cloud.

## v0.1.76

🚩 **The Pioneer badge**

- Everyone who plays the Steam app during early access receives the Pioneer badge, for good, with a reward screen right after the first setup.
- The badge appears next to your name in lobbies and on your profile.

## v0.1.75

⌨️ **Your keyboard's letters**

- Keys now show the letter printed on your keyboard: ZQSD on a French AZERTY keyboard instead of WASD, everywhere keys are shown (first launch, settings, help). Works in the Steam app and in Chrome or Edge.

## v0.1.74

🎛️ **A quicker first launch in the Steam app**

- The language Steam is set to is already picked: most players only have to press Continue.
- Two new steps follow: the sound, starting at half volume with a button to test it, then the main keys, which you can change before your first game. Everything else stays in the settings.

## v0.1.73

⚠️ **Moderation warnings you can see**

- When the moderation team gives your account an official warning, the game shows it the next time you open Territorium, with its reason. It stays on screen until you confirm with "I understand".

## v0.1.72

🛡️ **TGS, the Territorium Game Shield**

- The game servers now watch for abuse: a player who floods the server with commands (TGS-101), or whose game stays out of step with everyone else's (TGS-102), is disconnected with a clear message. After a desync you can rejoin the game straight away.
- TGS never bans anyone. Each disconnection goes to the team, who review the game's replay before deciding anything.

## v0.1.71

🎧 **Territorium on Discord**

- With the Steam app open, your Discord profile shows Territorium with its emblem: in the menu, in a lobby or in a game, the mode and map, the time played and the number of players. Your friends get a "Join the game" button.

## v0.1.70

- They can join your game from their Steam friends list, and the "Invite friends" button in a lobby opens Steam's invite window.
- Discord will show it too (with a "Join the game" button) as soon as the app's Discord link is set up.

## v0.1.16

🤝 **Regional alliances**

- In Nations games, an alliance with a neighbouring country (one you share a border with) lasts 50% longer: 7 minutes 30 instead of 5. Renewing it while you're still neighbours gives the same bonus.

🏳️ **Surrender as a vassal**

- In Nations games, once you have lost three quarters of your country, you can surrender to a player you are at war with from their player panel.
- If they accept, you become their vassal for 10 minutes: neither of you can attack the other, and you pay them 25% of your gold income. You stay in the game and can rebuild.
- Everyone is told who surrendered to whom. Breaking the deal counts as perjury, like breaking a peace treaty.
- AI countries accept a surrender unless it comes from a traitor.

## v0.1.8

🏳️ **Peace treaties**

- Two players at war can now sign peace without becoming allies. Right-click an enemy (or open their player panel) and choose the white flag. They get 15 seconds to sign or refuse.
- Once signed, neither side can attack the other for 3 minutes: land attacks turn back, boats sail home and missiles in flight between you are called off. Trade embargoes between you are lifted.
- Breaking the peace is perjury. It costs what betraying an ally costs (traitor mark, half defense for 30 seconds), every player is told, and you cannot offer peace to your victim again for 10 minutes. Firing a missile or an EMP at a player you are at peace with breaks it too.
- After a refusal, you must wait 60 seconds before asking that player again. When a peace runs out, either side can offer a new one after 30 seconds. You can only have one proposal waiting at a time.
- Nations weigh each offer: they refuse traitors, keep pressing a war they are winning, and gladly accept peace from a stronger player. Tribes accept every offer.
- The player panel shows how long the peace has left, and the timer ring on the "Break peace" button in the radial menu shows it too.

## v0.1.2

⚡ **EMP bomb**

- A new missile fired from a missile silo, for 1,500,000 gold (fixed price). Hotkey: `-`.
- It does no damage and takes no land. On impact it disables every enemy defense post, SAM launcher, missile silo, port and radar within 20 tiles for 15 seconds. Disabled structures pulse electric blue.
- A disabled SAM doesn't fire, a disabled silo can't launch, a disabled port sends no ships and a disabled defense post gives no defense bonus.
- Once a structure recovers, it is immune to EMPs for 30 seconds, so it can't be kept down forever.
- Your own and your allies' structures are never affected. SAM launchers can shoot an EMP down like any other missile.

📡 **Radar**

- A new structure: 250,000 gold for the first, +250,000 for each one you own, capped at 1,000,000. It takes 5 seconds to build. Hotkey: `=`.
- It sweeps twice per second and spots enemy boats, warships and missiles within 100 tiles. You get a message and a sonar ping, once per unit. Your own and allied units are ignored.
- An EMP blinds a radar while it is disabled.
